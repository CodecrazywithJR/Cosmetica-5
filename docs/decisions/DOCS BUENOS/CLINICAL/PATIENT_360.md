PATIENT 360 VIEW

Purpose
-------

The Patient 360 view provides a complete overview of a patient across all clinic activities.

Instead of navigating multiple modules, staff can see the entire patient history in a single interface.


Design Goals
------------

• fast navigation
• minimal clicks
• chronological understanding of patient history
• separation between clinical and financial information


Main Tabs
---------

Overview
Encounters
Proposals
Treatment Plans
Sales
Documents


Overview Tab
------------

Displays summary information.

Includes:

• patient demographics
• contact information
• alerts (missing consents, allergies)
• recent encounters
• upcoming appointments


Encounters Tab
--------------

Displays clinical history.

Each encounter shows:

• practitioner
• treatments performed
• notes
• clinical photos
• documents


Proposals Tab
-------------

Displays all proposals made to the patient.

Information:

• proposal status
• proposal date
• treatments offered
• total amount


Treatment Plans Tab
-------------------

Displays active and completed treatment plans.

Information:

• treatment name
• total sessions
• sessions completed
• sessions remaining


Sales Tab
---------

Displays financial history.

Information:

• sale amount
• invoice status
• payments received


Documents Tab
-------------

Displays uploaded documents.

Examples:

• consent forms
• medical reports
• scanned documents


Design Principles
-----------------

Patient 360 must remain readable and fast.

Rules:

• avoid overloaded screens
• show most recent activity first
• separate clinical from financial information
